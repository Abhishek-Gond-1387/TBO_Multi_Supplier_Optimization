💡 Multi-Supplier Inventory Matching Engine

Why Product Matching Matters for TBO

When TBO aggregates inventory from multiple suppliers (Hotelbeds, Agoda, Expedia, WebBeds, Viator & GetYourGuide), suppliers submit duplicate listings with slightly different spelling, addresses, and room codes. Our AI matching engine uses lexical similarity, geospatial distance, and room taxonomy normalization to consolidate duplicate listings into single, high-converting product pages while comparing rates across all suppliers.
