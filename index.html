Architecture & Methodology

About TBO Travel Intelligence

TBO Discovery is a Pan-India multi-supplier travel intelligence platform engineered to eliminate pricing asymmetries, ambiguous package inclusions, and mismatched travel plans.

Pricing Integrity Verification

Every displayed rate is tagged with strict transparent verification badges:
🟢 Live Verified: Polled directly against live audited supplier APIs (IRCTC, DGCA, live hotel tariffs, Open-Meteo).
🟡 Market Estimated: Benchmark model derived from real contracted seasonal tariffs.
⚪ Provider Unavailable: Displayed when remote suppliers are temporarily offline.

Genuine Destination Photography

All photographs displayed across our destination showcase and itineraries are genuine high-resolution imagery resolved via the Wikimedia Commons API and Wikipedia under Creative Commons licenses (CC-BY-SA, CC-BY, Public Domain), complete with author credits and licensing transparency.

Dynamic Party Sizing & Vehicle Allocation

Unlike rigid aggregator forms, TBO provides complete freedom: party sizes from 1 to 50+ travelers, durations from 1 to 60+ days, and automatic vehicle optimization (Sedan, Innova Crysta, 12-seater Tempo Traveller, or 20+ seat Volvo Coach).
